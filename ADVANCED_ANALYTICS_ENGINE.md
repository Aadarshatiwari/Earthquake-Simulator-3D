# Advanced analytics engine

Analytics preserves the previous Simulation charts in a collapsible panel. Protection Lab transfers its exact experiment into the research worker. It analyzes measured or synthetic input, transfers typed buffers, and downsamples only for display. Seven guided workflows cover spectra, isolation, tuned mass, soft storeys, stiffness, station comparison and convergence. Same-event comparisons use recorded UTC start offsets. Cross-event runs preserve independent sample intervals and record windows.
