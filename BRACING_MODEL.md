# Steel bracing model

With a pin-connected brace of area A, modulus E and length L, a small lateral drift δ induces the axial displacement `δ cos θ`. Projecting the axial force onto the horizontal axis gives `k = EA cos²θ/L`. Geometry derives L and θ from the storey rise and bay run. Single diagonals span one bay. V and inverted V legs use half the bay run. The stiffness is multiplied by the braced bay count and number of active legs. The pair may be represented by two linear legs or a one-active-leg idealization. K braces are visual only.

The resulting contributions add to the corresponding shear-storey spring. The pair idealization does not actively switch tension and compression legs during reversal. Buckling, yielding, failure, connection flexibility and redistribution are outside the model. The view displays geometry and storey response rather than member stress.
