# Graphics engine

The shared instanced Three.js viewport renders parametric geometry, procedural material textures, environment details, shadows and selectable architectural and structural views. The constructor and Earthquake Lab offer daytime, golden hour, overcast and neutral lighting, alongside Nepal-inspired urban, Terai and foothill settings. Four Nepal-inspired presets supplement the original set. The terrain explorer keeps its separate geographic rendering path.

Automatic, Low, Balanced, High and Ultra profiles adjust device pixel ratio and scene detail. Idle views render on demand. Home uses the same viewport and exposes a first-frame readiness signal. Model shapes remain stylized educational geometry. Lighting and camera controls affect presentation.
