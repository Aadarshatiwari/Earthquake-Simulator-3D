# Ground motion analysis

PGA is the maximum absolute acceleration. Arias intensity is π/(2g) times the trapezoidal integral of squared acceleration, with g=9.80665 m/s². RMS uses the same integral divided by duration before taking the square root. D5–95 and D5–75 interpolate cumulative crossings. Bracketed duration spans the first and last samples reaching 0.05 g by default. Zero input gives undefined percentile durations. [USGS methods](https://pubs.usgs.gov/of/2017/1008/ofr20171008.pdf).
