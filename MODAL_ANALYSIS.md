# Modal analysis

The solver derives modes from Kφ=ω²Mφ. Participation is Γ=(φᵀMr)/(φᵀMφ). Effective mass is (φᵀMr)²/(φᵀMφ). Percentage reference mass is rᵀMr. The modal panel shows frequency, periods, participation, effective and cumulative mass. The animated projection uses cosine phase and normalized shape, independent of earthquake time-history amplitude.
