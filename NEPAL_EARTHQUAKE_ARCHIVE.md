# Nepal earthquake archive

Open **Nepal archive** from the main navigation or add `?view=archive` to the local URL. Search by name, year or event ID. Expand the catalogue filters to constrain dates, magnitudes, depth, source, province, district or station-record availability. The timeline reflects those filters. Its counts describe the available catalogue and coverage varies by era.

Select an earthquake to inspect its origin, depth, provenance and supported products. Use **3D terrain** for the epicentre, hypocentre and depth section. Select **Replay earthquake** and choose a station and component for the measured signal. Gorkha and Dolakha have official USGS Atlas ShakeMap contours in the 2D map. The optional recent view uses the USGS public feed and stores the last successful result locally.

See [historical sources](HISTORICAL_EARTHQUAKE_SOURCES.md), [GIS architecture](NEPAL_GIS_ARCHITECTURE.md), [station data](STRONG_MOTION_DATASETS.md) and the [validation report](PHASE_4_VALIDATION_REPORT.md).
