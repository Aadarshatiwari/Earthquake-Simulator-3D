# Nepal engineering references

Nepal’s Department of Urban Development and Building Construction lists **NBC 105:2025 / 2082** for seismic design. It also lists **NBC 205:2024** for detailing low-rise reinforced concrete buildings without masonry infill and **NBC 202:2015 / NBC 203:2015** for masonry guidance. The department separately publishes retrofit guidance for adobe, masonry and reinforced concrete buildings. The index was checked in November 2026. [DUDBC building code index](https://dudbc.gov.np/pages/building-code/), [DUDBC retrofit guidance](https://www.dudbc.gov.np/pages/building-code-section/).

The ten Nepal-inspired scenarios are educational archetypes with illustrative physical inputs. They have no code-compliance assessment. Lessons cover load paths, regularity, seismic bands, vertical reinforcement, roof connections, soft-storey behaviour, construction quality and ductility. The app does not supply construction details.

The original Gorkha station accelerograms used by the lab are published separately from the building-code material. [Takai et al., 2016](https://doi.org/10.1186/s40623-016-0383-7), [bundle provenance](STRONG_MOTION_DATASETS.md).
