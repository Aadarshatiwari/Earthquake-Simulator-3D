# Numerical verification

Convergence compares Δt, Δt/2 and Δt/3 using the same piecewise-linear excitation. Peak and time-history differences reference the finest run on the original time grid. Gorkha ordinary-frame maximum roof history difference fell from 1.0511 mm at 0.01 s to 0.1643 mm at 0.005 s. The results test numerical refinement of the interpolated record and do not recover unmeasured motion. Independent constant-excitation SDOF peaks and Fourier tone power are automated reference checks.
