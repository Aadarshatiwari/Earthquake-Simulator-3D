# Pdf report generator

The browser lazily loads a compact PDF writer that produces vector plots from actual study results. The report includes time, version, source, intensity, case assumptions, modal periods, peak demand and energy. Fixed report sections and ASCII text are supported. The in-app preview uses the same study data. The [sample report](output/pdf/gorkha-research-report.pdf) uses verified Gorkha KTP NS samples and ordinary and isolated examples. Its three pages were rendered and inspected with Poppler.
