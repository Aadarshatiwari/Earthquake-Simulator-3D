# Seismic data provenance

The original bundled snapshot contains 273 ComCat events from 1900 through 2024. A separate 18-event ComCat extension uses the same query bounds and magnitude threshold from 1 January 2025 through 25 November 2026. The 291 instrumental entries are combined by stable event ID, with newer entries winning on identical IDs. Both snapshots contain records with preferred magnitude at least 5 in 26–31° N and 79–89° E. This query includes neighboring countries and supplies neither a complete record of Nepal earthquakes nor a uniform observation interval. Entries retain event IDs, preferred scale, coordinates, uncertainty notes where available and ComCat links. The archive includes two separately sourced pre-instrumental entries. [USGS FDSN Event API](https://earthquake.usgs.gov/fdsnws/event/1/), [ISC-GEM](https://www.isc.ac.uk/iscgem/).

USGS ShakeMap Atlas provides MMI, PGA in percent g, PGV in cm/s and 0.3-second PSA in percent g for two events. These are published estimated shaking fields. The archive packages generalized contours and links to the original product. USGS-authored public-domain material may include contributions with distinct rights at the source. [USGS ShakeMap product documentation](https://ghsc.code-pages.usgs.gov/esi/shakemap/docs2020/manual4_0/ug_products.html).

Geographic sources and licences are documented in [GIS architecture](NEPAL_GIS_ARCHITECTURE.md). Station file redistribution and handling are documented in [strong-motion datasets](STRONG_MOTION_DATASETS.md). `scripts/prepare-archive.py` records the transformations from downloaded source files. NEMRC, DMG and CESMD are reference resources. The four new Gorkha records come directly from the Takai et al. Figshare supplements, with file licences and checksums. The online recent view queries the last 30 days at M≥2.5 and shows the last successful update when available.
