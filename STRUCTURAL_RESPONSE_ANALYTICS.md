# Structural response analytics

The worker retains true full-resolution relative displacement and velocity, absolute floor acceleration, interstorey movement, drift and elastic restoring force. It separately reports support reaction, base-relative displacement, device travel and axial dashpot force. Peak time and height are computed at each DOF. Multi-case histories and energy use a common input-time cursor. Heatmaps are display samples and use a signed square-root scale or the documented PSD scale.
