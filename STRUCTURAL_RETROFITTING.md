# Structural retrofitting

Concrete and steel jacketing demonstrate explicit, user-selected changes to floor mass and storey stiffness. Soft-storey strengthening applies those changes at the first storey only. Brace and wall switches add their documented stiffness contributions. Seismic bands, masonry reinforcement and improved connections are educational geometry without a numerical capacity change.

The ordinary and retrofitted cases can receive the same synthetic or measured acceleration. Interpret differences as sensitivity to the entered idealized changes. Complete load paths, roof-to-wall connections, vertical reinforcement, continuity, material quality, foundations and workmanship remain essential. Model percentages are illustrative and cannot determine construction details or real building safety. [Nepal engineering references](NEPAL_ENGINEERING_REFERENCES.md).
